Our Story

More Than Just Coffee

Khalid Café was born from a simple belief: that great coffee and honest food can transform an ordinary moment into something memorable. Since 2018, we've been serving Solapur's coffee lovers with single-origin brews, freshly baked goods, and a kitchen that respects local flavours.

Every item on our menu is chosen with care — from the beans we roast in small batches, to the seasonal produce we source from nearby farms. Pull up a chair, order what you love, and stay as long as you like. That's the Khalid Café way.

Why We're Different

The Khalid Café Experience

We've thought about every detail so your visit feels effortless and special.

☕

Specialty Roasting

We roast our own beans in small weekly batches to guarantee peak freshness. Each origin is dialled in by our trained baristas for optimal extraction.

🍽️

All-Day Kitchen

Breakfast, brunch, lunch, and evening bites — our kitchen stays open from 8 AM to 10 PM so you're never too early or too late for a good meal.

💻

Work-Friendly Space

High-speed Wi-Fi, plentiful power sockets, quiet corners, and a steady stream of great coffee — the perfect place to get things done.

🎶

Curated Atmosphere

Handpicked playlists, warm lighting, and thoughtful décor make every visit feel like you've stepped out of the ordinary into something special.

🌱

Sustainable Choices

We use compostable packaging, source locally, and work to minimise waste. Good coffee shouldn't cost the planet.

📦

Easy Takeaway

Ordered ahead or walk in — our takeaway service is quick, accurate, and packaged to keep everything at its best until you open it.
